Testing this bridge

A test bridge from Sepolia to our LEZ 0.4 bridge test network. Test coins only. The network is reset now and then.

What you need

  1. Sepolia ETH in a browser wallet, such as MetaMask or Rabby.
  2. Logos Basecamp on your computer.
  3. The Names Wallet test build for LEZ 0.4: get the Names Wallet test build for LEZ 0.4.

A. From your Names Wallet (a deposit code)

In test builds that have “Receive from Ethereum”.

  1. In the Names Wallet, use “Receive from Ethereum” to get a deposit code.
  2. On the bridge page, choose “Paste deposit code” and paste it.
  3. Connect your Ethereum wallet on Sepolia, compare the digits with your Names Wallet, and deposit.
  4. Wait up to about 20 minutes.
  5. Your Names Wallet shows the deposit and offers the claim.
More about this way

The wallet makes and keeps the deposit’s secret; the bridge page sees only public facts. There is no claim file on this way. If the digits on the page differ from your wallet’s, stop: the code would deposit to someone else.

B. With a claim file

  1. On the bridge page, connect your Ethereum wallet on Sepolia.
  2. Choose the amount, then paste a public account whose keys are in your LEZ 0.4 wallet (Names Wallet 0.17).
  3. Save the claim file once, then deposit.
  4. Wait up to about 20 minutes.
  5. In the Names Wallet: Wallet → LEZ 0.4 → “Claim a bridge deposit”, choose the claim file, press “Claim” and approve it.
More about the claim file

The page makes the deposit’s secret in your browser tab and puts it in the claim file. Without the file the deposit cannot be claimed, and nobody can recover it for you. Whoever has it can claim the deposit: keep it private.

An account that is only in your LEZ 0.3 wallet cannot claim the deposit. A LEZ 0.4 wallet restored from the same recovery phrase has the same public accounts; one created new has different ones.

What to expect

  • The deposit is final on Sepolia in about 15 minutes. The claim works once our attester has posted the deposit root to LEZ 0.4: up to about 20 minutes after the deposit.
  • Installing a Names Wallet test build, Basecamp shows a notice that the package is unsigned.
  • In some builds, Approve becomes available only after a short wait.

Known limits

  • The deposit root reaches LEZ 0.4 through our operator key (our attester), not through a light client.
  • A public claim links the Sepolia deposit to the Logos account, publicly.
  • Private claims are not in the wallet yet.
  • The bridge’s relay pays the claim’s fee, so the account needs no LEZ. The relay has daily limits; when it will not pay, nothing is sent.
  • Withdrawals back to Ethereum are checked by proof on Ethereum. The first proofs are being set up; until then a burn waits.
  • Which bridge test network blocks count is the bridge operator’s word until a finality proof for that chain exists; the vault’s caps and a one-hour delay bound a wrong update.
  • Fixed amounts only: 0.01, 0.1 or 1 Sepolia ETH, or 100, 1,000 or 10,000 demo USDC (a test token only the team can hand out).
