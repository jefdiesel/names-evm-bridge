Security of the bridge page

There are two ways to deposit. They trade convenience for what this page ever sees.

Two ways to deposit

  • Made here (the default): the page creates your secret in this tab and puts it in the claim file. Convenient, but the secret exists in the browser until you close the tab.
  • From your Names Wallet’s deposit code: the wallet keeps the secret, and the page only sees public facts. The safer way, once the wallet’s bridge mini-app is out.

What can go wrong when the secret is made here

  • A tampered copy of this page could send the secret away and claim your deposit.
  • A browser extension allowed to read pages could read it too.

What protects you: the page’s Content-Security-Policy lets it talk to no site but its Sepolia read service (PublicNode); it loads no third-party code; it never stores or sends the secret; and every file is listed in a manifest you can check.

What the page checks before you sign

  • Your wallet is on Sepolia. Every other network, Ethereum mainnet included, is refused.
  • The vault is the known one and holds the configuration your deposit was made for.
  • The amount is one the vault accepts, deposits are open, the limit has room, and the commitment is new.
  • When the bridge fee is on: the deposit desk forwards to that vault and quotes the same amount, and the fee is in the total you see before your wallet asks.
  • The deposit succeeds when simulated, checked again right before your wallet asks.

A token approval is for the exact amount, never unlimited.

Your wallet is the last check

It must show Sepolia and the vault, 0xC2c6A059eAB928966B24FA5549d441A3B17B54Ed. When the bridge fee is on, it shows the deposit desk instead; the page shows the desk’s address before you confirm, and the desk passes the deposit to the vault in the same transaction. That catches a page that sends you to another vault. It cannot show a swapped commitment, so that depends on the page being genuine.

Limits today

  • Withdrawals back to Ethereum are checked by proof on Ethereum. The first proofs are being set up; until then a burn waits.
  • Which bridge test network blocks count is the bridge operator’s word until a finality proof for that chain exists; the vault’s caps and a one-hour delay bound a wrong update.
  • A claim is signed by the receiving account and needs the claim file. You claim in the Names Wallet (Wallet → LEZ 0.4 → “Claim a bridge deposit”), into a public account; that wallet build (0.17) is not released yet, so you can’t claim to your own account yourself today. The bridge pays a claim’s fee, so the account needs no LEZ. A claim to a public account links the Sepolia deposit to that account publicly.
  • A LEZ account ID has no checksum: a mistyped account, or a key pasted instead of an ID, is accepted and makes the deposit unclaimable. The page shows the account for checking and asks you to confirm it.
  • Tested with real Sepolia deposits through this page and MetaMask, and with a scripted wallet; not yet in Safari or Firefox, or with a screen reader.
  • Phone wallets work inside their own browser. There is no WalletConnect.
